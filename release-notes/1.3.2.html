Fifteen 1.3.2

Join meetings and shape the boxes beside the timer your way.

  • Events with a Zoom, Meet, Teams or other call link get a Join button. The heads-up before them joins with a click, too.
  • Click a reminder to tick it off, or an event to open it in your calendar. Hover for 💬 to make one your intention.
  • Priorities stand out in the Reminders box, with their colours inverted at the top of the list.
  • Drag a box's outer edge to make it wider or narrower, and switch on tucking it away behind the timer in Settings.
  • The boxes sit centred beside the timer, with rounded shadows, and your calendars are listed in one section by account.
