Fifteen 1.2.2

Blocked apps stay blocked, and your icon stays yours.

  • Blocked apps can no longer sneak back in from the Dock or ⌘-Tab while they're already open during focus.
  • Opening a blocked app now counts as one distraction, not two or three.
  • Your chosen app icon is kept when Fifteen updates.
  • "Pop up when a session ends" now lives in Display settings.
