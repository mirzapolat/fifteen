Fifteen 1.3.1

Your reminders, next to the timer.

  • Show a Reminders list beside the timer: tick things off, add new ones with +, and star today's priorities so they stay on top. Turn it on in Settings → Reminders.
  • Calendar and reminders can sit on either side of the timer, one on each.
  • Get a heads-up a few minutes before an event starts, even in the middle of a focus session.
  • Name the sessions Fifteen adds to your calendar with placeholders like {project} and {minutes}.
  • Calendar settings are tidier: your calendars come first, then subscribed ones, and each event has a button to open it in Calendar.
  • A small ✕ clears the intention field.
