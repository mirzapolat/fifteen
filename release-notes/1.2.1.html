Fifteen 1.2.1

Know what your group is up to, and keep Settings to what you use.

  • Link tells you exactly what changed, and who joined or left. A small popup drops from the menu bar, or slides out under the timer while it's open.
  • Choose whether Link shows a popup, a notification or both.
  • When a session ends, the ring pulses and jiggles instead of showing a dot.
  • Link and Insights have their own tabs in Settings, and each can be turned off completely. Projects and session ratings now live with Insights.
  • Fifteen tells you in Settings if it isn't allowed to send notifications, and helps you fix it.
  • Switch between Pomodoro and Flowtime in one click, and find app suggestions in their own menu.
