Fifteen 1.1

A warmer welcome, and a few small things made better.

  • A welcome screen on first launch, and this What's New window after each update.
  • Fifteen now opens at login. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • New menu bar option: Icon or Time, which shows the icon while the timer rests and the time while it runs.
  • Deleting a timer preset no longer makes Fifteen quit.
