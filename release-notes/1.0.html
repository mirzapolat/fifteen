Fifteen 1.0

One timer. One thing. The first release of Fifteen for Mac.

  • A calm Pomodoro timer in your menu bar, in a Liquid Glass bubble.
  • Link: focus together with friends on one shared timer.
  • Hide distracting apps, Do Not Disturb while you focus, and a break curtain.
  • Analytics with goals, a year heatmap, projects and export.
  • Updates now arrive automatically.
