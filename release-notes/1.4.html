Fifteen 1.4

Simpler, with less to hunt for.

  • "What's the one thing?" is always under the ring while you focus. No more button to reveal it.
  • Click an event next to the timer to focus on it, make it your intention or join its call. Tuck either box away with the arrow in its header.
  • Calendar and Reminders wait as a slim strip until Fifteen may read them. Click it to connect.
  • Blocking an app is one "Add App" menu: apps that are open, suggestions, or any app you choose.
  • A break that starts by itself while your Mac sleeps now runs from the moment focus ended.
  • "Add Time" says what it does, and buttons no longer look greyed out in windows in the background.
