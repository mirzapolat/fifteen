Fifteen 1.3

Your calendar, next to the timer.

  • See today's events beside the timer, and add your finished focus sessions to a calendar you pick. Turn it on in Settings → Calendar.
  • Fifteen now asks before it quits, so ⌘Q can't end your blocking by accident. You can switch this off in Settings → General.
  • You can change your blocked apps and websites during a break. They stay locked while you focus.
  • Keeping the timer counting after a session no longer hides the rating. You can rate it while the extra time counts up.
