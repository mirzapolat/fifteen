Fifteen 1.3.3

A little more room to breathe beside the timer.

  • Tucking the calendar and reminders away from their edge is now on from the start. Switch it off in Settings if you'd rather not.
  • The tab for tucking a box away no longer covers your events or reminders.
  • Titles and reminders keep the same distance from the timer as your events.
  • Priorities in the Reminders box glow softly in the list's colour again.
  • The intention switch moved to Settings → Insights, right next to Projects.
