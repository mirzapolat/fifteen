Fifteen 1.3.5

A friendlier start, and reminders that feel better to tick off.

  • New installs get a short setup: pick your rhythm, sounds, calendar and reminders, the apps and websites to block, your app icon, and how to focus together with Link.
  • Click a reminder to open it in Reminders. Only the circle marks it done, now with a little burst when you tick it off.
  • Click the list's name in the reminders box to show another list, and connecting Reminders now shows your list right away.
  • The calendar and reminders lists fade softly at the edges instead of being cut off, and reminders no longer shift when you hover them.
  • No more play button on calendar events and reminders. Right-click one to focus on it.
  • What's New stays on top, so it no longer hides behind other windows.
  • The menu bar now shows the icon while the timer is unused and the time while it's in use. Change it in Settings → Display.
  • Settings → About links to getfifteen.de.
