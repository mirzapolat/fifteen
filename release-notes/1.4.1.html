Fifteen 1.4.1

Your side boxes, your size.

  • Resize the calendar and reminders boxes again: drag a box's outer edge. Double-click the edge for the usual width. Fifteen remembers the size you pick.
  • Fixed a bug where, after a click in the wrong spot, the timer turned invisible and the menu bar stopped responding.
  • The calendar and reminders boxes no longer have a button that only opens the Calendar or Reminders app. Click an event or a reminder to open it there.
