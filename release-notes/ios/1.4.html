Fifteen 1.4

Your day, next to the timer.

  • Calendar See today's events from the top of the timer and tap one to focus on it, or to join its call. Fifteen warns you when a session would run into your next event, and can make it fit.
  • Reminders Keep a Reminders list a tap away: tick things off, add new ones, pick today's priorities, or turn one into your intention.
  • Backwards tracking Finished focus sessions can be added to a calendar you pick, with your notes and rating.
  • Breaks that keep time A break that starts by itself while your iPhone is locked now runs from the moment focus ended, and you'll get a notification when it's over.
  • Notes that stay If iOS closes Fifteen right after a session, the note and rating are still waiting for you when you come back.
