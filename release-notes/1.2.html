Fifteen 1.2

Distracting websites now wait until your session ends.

  • New Website Blocker in Safari, Chrome and Firefox: blocked sites show a quiet page during focus, with a way to allow one for 5 minutes.
  • Choose a block list or an allow list, and whether each site includes its subdomains.
  • Pick which browsers Fifteen watches. Each one shows right there if it still needs your permission.
  • Two new app icons: 15 Rainbow and Ring Rainbow.
  • A new Insights icon, and a calmer Focus tab in Settings.
