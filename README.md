# Fifteen

**Simple, free Pomodoro timer.**

Fifteen is a feature-rich, free and open‑source Pomodoro timer to keep you focused, day in, day out.
Explore what it has to offer!

[**Download for Mac**](https://getfifteen.de/download/mac) · Download for iOS (soon)

Available for macOS 26 or later · [getfifteen.de](https://getfifteen.de)

## Mac

The Mac app sits quietly in your menu bar and stays out of your way while you work.
Click it to open the controls.

- Shows the time, a small ring, or both in the menu bar
- <kbd>⌃⌥P</kbd> starts or pauses from any app, <kbd>⌃⌥O</kbd> opens Fifteen
- Hides distracting apps while you focus
- Optional full-screen break with a slow breathing circle
- Turns on Do Not Disturb during focus
- Runs your Shortcuts when focus starts or ends
- Pauses when your Mac locks or sleeps
- Opens at login and keeps itself up to date

## iPhone & iPad *(coming soon)*

Soon you’ll be able to use the companion app on iPhone and iPad to track your time, even when

## Features

- **Pomodoro.** Everything you’d expect from a classic Pomodoro timer, and nothing less.
- **Flowtime.** With Flowtime, your session isn’t limited by a timer. Stop whenever you want, and
  Fifteen works out the right amount of rest.
- **Flow mode.** In the zone when the bell rings? Keep going. The extra minutes still count.
- **Intentions & projects.** Write down the one thing you’re working on and file it under a project.
- **Rate your sessions.** After each session, rate it with up to five stars and add a short note to
  look back on later.
- **Sounds & haptics.** A classic bell, a singing bowl, a wood block, a ticking clock, white noise
  and rain, plus haptic feedback on iPhone.
- **Notifications.** Fifteen lets you know when it’s time for a break, so you never miss one.
- **Siri & Shortcuts.** Start, pause or ask how much time is left. Hook Fifteen into your own automations.
- **Private.** No account, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays on your device.

## Your Insights

- **Streaks.** Daily streaks with rest days, if you want them.
- **Heatmap.** Every focused day of the year on one calendar.
- **Best days & hours.** See which weekdays and times of day you focus best.
- **Goals.** Set a daily and weekly focus goal and track it.
- **Session log.** Time, length, project, rating and notes for every session.
- **Projects.** Organize sessions by project and see how your time splits.
- **Import & export.** Export to CSV or a full backup and restore it any time. Your data never
  leaves your hands.

## Link

Focus with friends or your team. Start a group, share the six-letter code, and everyone runs on
the same timer, on Mac and iPhone. Only the timer is shared.
